How fast does the flatbed tow truck arrive in Miami?

Our average arrival time is 15 to 20 minutes across Miami Design District, I-95, Downtown, Brickell, South Beach, and Palmetto Expressway. We keep standby flatbeds stationed strategically 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Can you tow sports cars with low ground clearance without scraping?

Yes! We specialize in zero-degree hydraulic ground-drop rollback decks with extended race ramps specifically engineered for Porsche 911 GT3, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Corvette, and lowered track cars without any front lip or diffuser contact.

Do you provide receipts for insurance / AAA roadside reimbursement?

Absolutely. We provide itemized digital receipts with our Florida State Commercial Carrier permit and USDOT license numbers, allowing you to submit for 100% reimbursement from State Farm, Geico, Progressive, AAA, or American Express roadside policies.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), Apple Pay, Google Pay, Cash, and corporate fleet accounts upon delivery.
